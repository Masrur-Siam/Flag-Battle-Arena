12 COMPETITION MODES
🤼 Sumo Battle

Push competitors outside a raised circular arena ring. Last flag inside wins.

🏃 Obstacle Race

Navigate through rotating windmill hammers, hurdles, and lateral bumpers to reach the finish line.

🕳 Last Flag Standing

Floor tiles shake, flash red, and collapse into the abyss. Leap to safe tiles and survive.

🪢 Tug of War

Two teams battle with physics-based rope pulling across a mud pit trap. Rhythm bursts decide the victor.

🌋 Rising Lava

Molten lava rises from below! Flags must continuously jump to higher stepped platforms.

🎳 Bowling Challenge

Charge down the polished lane and knock down standing pins. Most pin knockdowns wins.

🧗 Jump Challenge

Ascending floating pillars across a deep canyon. Test jump timing, height, and landing balance.

⛷ Downhill Race

Roll down a steep physics slope with jumps, banked curves, and speed boosters.

🧊 Glass Bridge

Cross paired glass tiles. One tile holds firm; the other shatters. Remember safe steps!

🌀 Spin Battle

Spinning bumper flags collide at extreme angular speeds to launch rivals out of the bowl.

🗼 Tower Climb

Spiral obstacle tower with rotating sweeper arms. Climb the stairs to reach the summit crown.

⚽ Football Battle

Autonomous team soccer with turf pitch, goal nets, goalkeepers, and physics ball striking.
